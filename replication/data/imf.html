Overview

  • Primary Commodity Prices data are updated monthly based on the IMF’s Primary Commodity Prices System.
  • The database includes country-specific commodity-price based indices for 182 economies from 1962.
  • For each country, the change in the international price of up to 45 individual commodities is weighted using commodity-level trade data.

Data and Coverage

  • Frequency: Monthly updates.
  • Coverage:
    • 182 economies.
    • Time span starting from 1962.
    • Up to 45 individual commodities per country, weighted by commodity-level trade data.
  • Indices: Country-specific commodity-price based indices constructed from international commodity price changes and trade weights.
