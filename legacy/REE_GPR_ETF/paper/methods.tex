\subsection{Geopolitical Risk and Rare-Earth-Related Equity Funds}
We examine whether changes in the news-based Geopolitical Risk (GPR) index of
\citet{caldara_iacoviello_2022} are associated with the monthly returns of
rare-earth-related exchange-traded funds. Our principal asset is the VanEck Rare
Earth and Strategic Metals ETF (REMX). Other strategic-materials ETFs are used
as shorter-history comparison funds; the market and metals/mining proxies are
SPY and XME, respectively. We use monthly USD adjusted ETF close prices and
match them to the official monthly GPR series.

For ETF $i$, define the log return
$r_{i,t}=100[\log(P_{i,t})-\log(P_{i,t-1})]$ and a standardized GPR change
$z_t=\Delta\log(1+\mathrm{GPR}_t)/\widehat{\sigma}_{\Delta\log(1+\mathrm{GPR})}$.
The baseline contemporaneous specification is
\begin{equation}
 r_{i,t}=\alpha_i+\beta_i z_t+\gamma_i r_{\mathrm{SPY},t}
 +\delta_i r_{\mathrm{XME},t}+\varepsilon_{i,t}.
\end{equation}
To examine predictive content, rather than same-period association, we estimate
\begin{equation}
 r_{i,t+1}=a_i+\theta_i z_t+\lambda_i r_{i,t}
 +\mu_i r_{\mathrm{SPY},t}+\nu_i r_{\mathrm{XME},t}+u_{i,t+1}.
\end{equation}
Both regressions use heteroskedasticity and autocorrelation-consistent
(Newey--West) standard errors with three monthly lags. We report the
conditional mean returns during top-decile increases in the GPR index and
cumulative returns over the subsequent one- and three-month horizons, enforcing
nonoverlapping event windows. These regressions describe statistical
associations and predictive relationships, not causally identified effects.

\paragraph{Limitations.} Rare-earth ETFs hold equities exposed to a wider set
of materials, not spot rare-earth commodities. In addition, the Global X fund
changed its investment strategy and ticker (DMAT to EART) in March 2026;
returns through March 2026 are excluded from our EART subsample. The recent
inception of several relevant ETFs limits statistical power. Monthly index
publication delays and revisions preclude unqualified real-time trading claims.
